Support

Questions about Wayfarer's Ledger, and how to reach us.

How do I log a payment?

On the home screen, tap Record, type the amount, pick a purpose and tap Record. Date and note are optional.

I made a mistake

Right after you log it, tap Undo on the note that appears. Later, open the Ledger and tap the entry to change or delete it.

Salary, rent and other regular items

Settings → Recording → Recurring. They are logged for you on the day.

A daily limit or a saving goal

Settings → Goals. Both are optional.

Exporting a month's entries

In the Ledger, Export this month (the share button at the top) saves the month on show as a spreadsheet (CSV).

A new iPhone

Your entries stay on the device. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud backup or a computer backup of the old one, and the app's data comes along.

Apple Watch

On your iPhone, open the Watch app, find Wayfarer's Ledger under Available Apps and tap Install. Set the watch buttons in Settings → Recording → Watch buttons.

Restoring a purchase

On the Wayfarer's Ledger Complete page, tap Restore purchases.

Language

The app follows your device's language: English, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Français or Español.
