Privacy Policy

Wayfarer's Ledger is a budget journal that keeps everything on your device. We do not collect any data about you.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What the app stores

Your entries (amount, purpose, date and note), your settings, recurring items, your own purposes and your achievements are kept in the app on this device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or crash reporting.

Apple Watch

The watch app and your iPhone pass entries to each other directly through Apple's WatchConnectivity. Nothing goes through a server of ours.

Exporting

When you export a month's entries, the app makes a spreadsheet file and hands it to the system's share sheet. You choose where it goes.

Purchases

The complete version and tips are in-app purchases made through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Backups and deleting

Your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) may include the app's data, under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes everything it kept on the device.

Children

We collect no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will say so, with the new date.
